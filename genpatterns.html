Definition

For this semester-long discovery project, we must choose a feeling. My chosen feeling is “curious”. Curious means wanting to learn, making you question and think, and wanting to explore something. Curiosity can be both negative and positive depending on context. Sometimes, things can be strange and cause you to be curious, or something sparks your interest, and you become curious.

Color Association

Chartreuse: It communicates boldness, youth, vitality, and creativity. Butter: happiness without danger, sunshine, optimism, and happiness in most cultures. Orange: energy, youth, and happiness; the creative center/second chakra in Eastern philosophy. Primary colors together invoke curiosity.
